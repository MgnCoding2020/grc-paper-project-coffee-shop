# Privileged User Agreement
Burn and Churn Coffee

**Supports:** PS-6 (access agreements), AC-6 (least privilege) | **Evidence:** E-20 | **Signed by:** anyone who holds an admin account (the Owner and the IT Technician)

This agreement is signed in addition to the [Acceptable Use Policy](../../04-policies/P-002-acceptable-use.md) acknowledgment, before any admin access is granted, and again every year.

---

## Rules for Admin Accounts

I agree that:

1. I use my **admin account** (`adm-first.last`) only for admin tasks, and my **everyday account** for email, browsing and everything else.
2. I never share my admin account, my password or my MFA method, and I never sign in as anyone else.
3. I protect my admin account with the strongest MFA method available (phishing-resistant where supported).
4. I use only the admin roles my job needs. I ask for more through a change record, and give them up when the task is done.
5. I don't change firewall rules, admin roles or in-scope systems without a change record ([change management](../../09-change-management/README.md)), except in an emergency, which I record within one business day.
6. I don't approve my own access requests or certify my own access in an access review.
7. I keep shared credentials only in the company password manager ([PR-009](../../05-procedures/PR-009-credential-vault.md)), never in notes, email or a browser.
8. I don't read employees' email, files or messages unless the Owner authorizes it in writing for a specific investigation.
9. I report a suspected compromise of any admin account or shared credential **immediately** to the Owner.
10. When I leave the role, I hand over my work and accept that my accounts are disabled and the credentials I knew are rotated the same day.

I understand that admin activity is logged and reviewed, and that breaking these rules may lead to loss of access and disciplinary action.
